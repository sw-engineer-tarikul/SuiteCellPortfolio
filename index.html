Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the SuiteCell platform, architecture, and private beta rollout.

Traditional ERPs (like SAP or NetSuite) were designed in the 1990s as monolithic, slow relational databases requiring 12-month consultant rollouts. SuiteCell is built on a modern cellular micro-ledger: each functional area (CRM, Finance, HR, Inventory) functions as an autonomous yet instantly synchronized cell. You get modern UI, instant setup, built-in AI agents, and sub-second performance without hiring an army of consultants.

You can start modularly! Many early adopters start with Cell Flow (CRM) or Cell Books (Finance) while keeping their existing systems, using our live bi-directional connectors. Once your team experiences the unified experience, you can toggle on remaining cells with zero downtime.

Invites are released in weekly batches starting Q4 2026. Queue position is based on signup order, team size alignment with active beta tracks, and referrals. Sharing your unique referral link after joining moves you up 100 spots per signup.

Security is baked into our foundation. SuiteCell features customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK), end-to-end 256-bit AES encryption in transit and at rest, SOC2 Type II certification, GDPR/CCPA compliance, and dedicated regional data residency zones. We never use your company data to train public foundation models.

We believe in transparent, per-user pricing with all core cells included. No hidden maintenance charges, API rate-limit penalties, or extortionate user tiers. Early access members who join the waitlist receive an exclusive 50% lifetime discount applied to whichever plan they choose.
